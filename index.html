Expanding Marine Protections

NRDC was working to strengthen the network of marine protected areas and establish two new MPAs, Point Sal and Mishopshno, to increase connectivity. In order to turn our educational outreach efforts into action, I wrote a series of direct outreach emails and SMS blasts to NRDC members encouraging them to send messages to Fish and Wildlife Commissioner. In total, we sent 7,045 emails to commissioners.

Urban Sustainability

LES Ecology Center SEO + Data Systems Overhaul

As a local non-profit that is deeply embedded in its community, the LES Ecology Center is a hub of environmental and sustainability resources for New York City. The organization offers an array of services from composting to e-waste recycling, as well as workshops and volunteer opportunities that bring the community together. While the in-person reputation of the Ecology Center was strong, their digital reach was limited, with relatively low traffic to the website (the primary hub for communicating events and services) and no systems in place for accurate and comprehensive tracking.

I led a major overhaul to implement industry-wide best practices and create a sophisticated infrastructure on par with significantly larger organizations. In order to get an accurate picture of web traffic, I set up a UTM tracking protocol that categorized all traffic based on source, medium, and campaign within Google Analytics. This helped the organization better understand what outreach methods were most successful in driving traffic. I also set up a Google Search Console to start tracking specific organic search data. In order to create greater transparency around web data, I designed a custom data dashboard in Looker Studio, which was published on a Communications Intranet that I also built so all staff had 24/7 access to that information.

After establishing the data infrastructure, I created an SEO plan to increase organic traffic to the site. I started by switching their event sign ups to be hosted on the website instead of on eventbrite to ensure all traffic for their events was not lost to third party sites, thereby increasing search rankings and giving their audience more opportunities to engaged sessions with multiple page visits. I also set up a website blog so that staff could share their expertise, while also answering high volume search queries that would bring more new visitors to the site.

This project drove traffic up by 143% in just a year. The value of this increased traffic is significant as it means there are more attendees at workshops, more volunteers, more donors, and more people taking advantage of the environmental services offered.
